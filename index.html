The night before Christmas. Something is in the chimney.

'Twas the night before Christmas, and all through the house
Not a creature was stirring, not even the mouse.
The stockings hung limp, and a little bit wet,
And the soot on the hearth was still warm. Not yet cold.

Welcome, traveller

You found my little corner of the web on the one night of the year when nobody should be awake. Stay if you like. Keep the lights low, and do not look at the fireplace.

Before you go to bed

Leave out the cookies and the milk. Lock the door. Count the stockings twice. If you hear bells, they are probably sleigh bells.

Probably.

The cookies are gone. So is the dog. Merry Christmas.
